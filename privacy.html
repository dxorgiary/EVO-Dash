Privacy Policy — EVO Dash

Last updated: August 2026

EVO Dash ("the App") is developed by DS DEVELOPER ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

1. No Personal Data Collected

EVO Dash is a fully offline game. It does not require an internet connection to play, does not ask you to create an account, and does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, location, or contacts.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

The App saves your game progress — including coins, unlocked animals, high scores, and settings — locally on your own device using standard on-device storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not sent to us or to any third party
  • Is automatically removed if you uninstall the App or clear its app data

3. No Third-Party Services

EVO Dash does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking software. We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

4. Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive device permissions beyond what is required to install and run the App.

5. Children's Privacy

Since the App collects no personal data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any other age group.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the revised version will be posted at this same web address with an updated "Last updated" date.
